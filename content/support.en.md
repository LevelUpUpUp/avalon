# Avalon Support

Avalon is developed and provided by {{developer_name}}. It manages local photos and videos on a Mac and allows authorized users to access the library through a mobile browser on the local network.

## Contact us

For technical support, feedback or privacy requests: [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}})

Include the app version, macOS version, steps leading to the problem and the error message. Privacy and support in the app lets you copy version and platform information. Do not send passwords, session credentials, full private photos or your complete library. Redact personal information, file paths and private content before sending screenshots or logs.

## Your phone cannot connect

Check that the Mac and phone are on the same trusted local network, Avalon and its local services are running, and system local-network permissions and firewall settings allow access. The default connection uses HTTP. Do not expose service ports directly to the public internet to connect your phone. Contact the local administrator about sign-in or library permissions.

## Files are inaccessible

Check that external disks are connected and review Folder access in the app. If needed, grant access again through the system folder picker. Review applicable permissions in macOS System Settings. Do not delete source files or the library just to troubleshoot access.

## Account and library deletion

Privacy and support offers Delete my account, and local administrators can Reset local library. Their retention scopes differ: deleting an account retains shared originals, while resetting a library also retains source files, uploaded originals and backups. Read the full privacy explanation before confirming. After account deletion, keep the service running until background cleanup finishes.
